# Victoria Reindale — Website Management Guide
**A step-by-step handbook for Victoria and her management team.**

---

## 1. Accessing Your Dashboard

You do not need to install any software or understand coding to update your website. Everything is managed directly through your web browser.

1. Open your browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, etc.) and go to:
   ```text
   https://victoriareindalesoprano.com/admin
   ```
2. Enter your **Admin Password** and click **"Se connecter"** (Log In).
3. Once logged in, you will see a clean dashboard with metric counters and clear tabs.

> **Tip**: At any time, click the **"Voir le site"** (View site) button in the top navigation bar to open your live website in a new tab and check how your updates look.

---

## 2. Managing Concerts & Events ("Concerts & Événements")

This tab allows you to add upcoming performances, edit details, or remove cancelled concerts.

### How to Add a New Event:
1. Click the **"Ajouter un événement"** (Add an Event) button.
2. Fill out the fields:
   - **Titre (FR & EN)**: The name of the recital, opera, or concert (e.g., *"Récital d'Opéra — Mozart & Bellini"*).
   - **Date & Heure**: Select the date and time of the performance.
   - **Lieu / Salle**: Name of the church, cathedral, theater, or venue (e.g., *"Cathédrale Sainte-Cécile"*).
   - **Ville & Pays**: City and Country (e.g., *"Albi, France"*).
   - **Billetterie (Ticket Link)**: If tickets are sold online, paste the booking URL (e.g., Fnac Spectacles, Ticketmaster, or direct venue link).
   - **Affiche / Photo**: Upload the concert poster or photo.
3. Click **"Enregistrer l'événement"** (Save Event).

### Good to Know:
- **Automatic Archiving & Cleanup**: You don't need to manually delete old concerts! 24 hours after a concert has passed, our system automatically removes it and frees up storage space.
- **Private Events**: Check the *"Événement privé"* box if Victoria is booked for a private wedding or gala and you do not want public venue or ticket details displayed.

## 3. Updating Profile, Bios & Photos ("Profil & Biographie")

Use this tab to update Victoria's presentation texts, headlines, photos, and contact links.

### 1. Welcome Headlines (Hero Section)
- **Titre principal**: The main name or header shown on the home screen.
- **Sous-titre**: Victoria's artistic title (e.g., *"Soprano · Artiste Vocale"* in French, *"Soprano · Vocal Artist"* in English).

### 2. Main Photos
- **Photo d'accueil (Hero Photo)**: Victoria's large portrait that visitors first see when landing on the site.
- **Photo de profil (À propos)**: The portrait shown in the "About" biography section.

> **Recommended Image Guidelines**:
> - **Formats**: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, or `.webp`.
> - **File Size**: Between **1 MB and 3 MB**. Avoid uploading massive 20 MB camera raw files directly, as they can slow down mobile visitors.

### 3. Biography Texts
- You can edit both the **French** and **English** biographies independently. Paragraphs and line breaks will display exactly as written.

### 4. Direct Contact & Social Links
- Update Victoria's official contact email address and phone number.
- Paste links to her **Instagram**, **YouTube**, and **Spotify** channels. Icons will automatically appear across the site.

Click **"Enregistrer les modifications"** at the top right to save your changes.

---

## 4. Managing Musical Packages & Rates ("Formules & Tarifs")

In the **"Formules & Tarifs"** tab, you can customize the services Victoria offers for private bookings (e.g., weddings, funerals, private concerts).

### For each service package, you can:
- **Set Title & Description** in French and English.
- **Display Starting Price**: e.g., *"À partir de 450 €"* or *"Sur devis"*.
- **Occasions / Included items**: List what the package covers (e.g., *"Cérémonie religieuse, Vin d'honneur, Ave Maria"*).
- **Hide or Show**: Toggle visibility without deleting the package.

---

## 5. Photo Gallery ("Galerie Photo")

Showcase high-resolution photography from Victoria's performances.

1. Click **"Ajouter une photo"**.
2. Select your photo file.
3. Choose a category:
   - **Concert & Récital** (Concerts & Recitals)
   - **Cérémonie & Mariage** (Weddings & Ceremonies)
   - **Portrait** (Artistic Portraits)
4. Add a short title or caption (optional).
5. Click **"Ajouter à la galerie"**.

To remove an old photo, simply click the red **"Supprimer"** button on the photo card.

---

## 6. Video Recordings ("Enregistrements Vidéo")

Embed YouTube recordings directly on the site so visitors can listen to Victoria's voice.

1. Copy the URL of any video from Victoria's YouTube channel:
   ```text
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XXXXXXXXXXX
   ```
2. In the **"Enregistrements Vidéo"** tab, click **"Ajouter une vidéo"**.
3. Paste the YouTube URL into the field.
4. Give it a title (e.g., *"Puccini — O Mio Babbino Caro"*).
5. Click **"Enregistrer"**. The video player is embedded on the site with no external ads or third-party tracking.

---

## 7. How Inquiries & Bookings Work

When a client wants to book Victoria or asks for a quote:
- The website contact form uses a **direct email link (`mailto:`)**.
- When the visitor clicks **"Envoyer ma demande"**, their email program (Apple Mail, Outlook, Gmail app) opens with Victoria's address prefilled and their message formatted.
- If the visitor uses webmail in a browser, the site provides a **"Copier les informations"** button so they can paste it directly into Gmail or Yahoo.
- **All inquiries go straight to Victoria's inbox**, with no third-party email servers in between.
